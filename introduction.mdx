# HAAT partner docs

Use these kits when onboarding a POS or delivery partner to HAAT.

## Which kit?

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Market" icon="store" href="/kits/market/overview" cta="Open Market kit" arrow="true">
    Grocery / market catalogs, promotions, order intake, and price sync.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Restaurant" icon="utensils" href="/kits/restaurant/overview" cta="Open Restaurant kit" arrow="true">
    Restaurant menus (meals, addons, deals) and real-time order create.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Advanced API" icon="plug" href="/kits/advanced-api/overview" cta="Open Advanced API kit" arrow="true">
    Partner pushes business status, order status, hours, and availability into HAAT.
  </Card>
  <Card title="DaaS" icon="truck" href="/kits/daas/overview" cta="Open DaaS kit" arrow="true">
    Delivery partners validate and submit delivery orders to HAAT.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Menu builders" icon="clipboard-list" href="/builders/overview" cta="Open builder guide" arrow="true">
    Internal POS guide for Market and Restaurant Content — PosId, barcodes, and what each POS syncs.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## How to use a kit

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read Overview">
    Confirm this is the right kit for the partner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Setup">
    Share credentials and environment URLs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Implement action pages">
    Follow each how-to page (menu, orders, sync, etc.).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go-live checklist">
    Tick every item on the kit checklist before production.
  </Step>
</Steps>

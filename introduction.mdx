## How to use a kit

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read Overview">
    Confirm this is the right kit for the partner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Setup">
    Share credentials and environment URLs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Implement action pages">
    Follow each how-to page (menu, orders, sync, etc.).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go-live checklist">
    Tick every item on the kit checklist before production.
  </Step>
</Steps>

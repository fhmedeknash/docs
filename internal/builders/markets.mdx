# Market builders

Markets use **barcode** and **product code**. There is no PosId table. Read [Matching keys](/builders/matching-keys) if that is new.

Most market POS types **update products that already exist**, and they create or update **deals**. They usually **do not** add brand-new products. The exception is **Comax**, which can add new products.

<Warning>
  There is no safety net. One extra space or a missing zero on the barcode will not show a red error. The order simply will not match on their side.
</Warning>

## Your steps on every market

<Steps>
  <Step title="Which POS is it?">
    Comax, All4Shops, Aviv, Barak — or a fully manual catalog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get their file">
    CSV or JSON. Copy barcode and product code **exactly**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build or align the HAAT catalog">
    Do not invent extra product codes if this POS only updates existing products.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill both codes">
    **Barcode** finds the product for Comax and Aviv. **Product code** finds the product for All4Shops and Barak — and it is what **deals** use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a test order">
    Confirm the same products appear on their side.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Comax

**What it does**

- They can **add** new products.
- They can **update** price and availability on products that already exist.
- We pull **deals** every day at **05:00** (and you can ask for an extra pull from backoffice).

**Match on:** barcode **or** product code for products. **Product code** for deals.

**What you do**

- New products may appear from Comax. Still check the codes on anything you add by hand.
- If a deal does not show, the product code does not match.

## All4Shops

**What it does**

- They send **price and availability** only. They **cannot** create a new product.
- We pull barcodes every day at **01:00** and email a **mismatch report**.
- We pull deals every day at **05:00**.

**Match on:** **product code** for everything.

**What you do**

- Create the HAAT product **first**. Then their price can land on it.
- Open the mismatch email. Those rows will never update until you fix the product code.

## Aviv

**What it does**

- **We** download their CSV files.
- Product file and deals file: **once a day**.
- Price file: **every 2 hours**.
- Updates price, availability, and product details. Creates/updates deals.

**Match on:** **barcode** for products. **Product code** for deals.

**What you do**

- Build from **their** file. Do not make up a second set of codes.
- Prices are only as new as the last 2-hour file — not live.

Aviv is also a restaurant POS (mapping + orders only). See [Restaurant builders](/builders/restaurants).

## Barak

**What it does**

- **We** download their CSV.
- After we read a file, it is moved to an **OLD** folder on their side so we do not read it twice.
- Updates price/availability and deals.

**Match on:** **product code** (they call it `ItemCode`).

**What you do**

- HAAT `ItemCode` must match their file.
- If they say “we already sent the file,” it may already be in **OLD**. Ask for a **new** file, or a pull from backoffice.

## Remember

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Copy codes exactly">
    Wrong barcode or product code = the product is “not found.” You will not always see an error in HAAT.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Most POS types cannot create new products">
    Only Comax is expected to add new SKUs. All4Shops will never create a product for you.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Deals use product code">
    Even when the product itself matches on barcode (Aviv, Comax).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Aviv and Barak are not real-time">
    They send files on a schedule. If a price looks old, wait for the next file or ask for a pull.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Read the All4Shops mismatch email">
    Unfixed rows will never get a price update.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

---
title: "Update availability"
description: "Turn menu items available or unavailable in bulk."
api: "PUT http://ext-integration.haat-delivery.io/api/pos/business/{businessId}/availability"
---

`items` are your item identifiers as agreed in onboarding.

**Example:**

<pre class="redoc-example"><code>X-Api-Key: YOUR_API_KEY</code></pre>

<div class="redoc-schema-label">REQUEST BODY SCHEMA: <strong>application/json</strong></div>

<div class="redoc-tree">
  <div class="redoc-field">
    <div class="redoc-field-name"><code>items</code><span class="redoc-required">required</span></div>
    <div class="redoc-field-meta"><div class="redoc-type">string[]</div><p>Item ids, for example <code>ITEM-001</code>.</p></div>
  </div>
  <div class="redoc-field">
    <div class="redoc-field-name"><code>available</code><span class="redoc-required">required</span></div>
    <div class="redoc-field-meta"><div class="redoc-type">boolean</div><p><code>true</code> = sell, <code>false</code> = out of stock.</p></div>
  </div>
</div>

## Responses

<details class="redoc-response" open>
  <summary><span class="redoc-code">200</span> Success or queued</summary>
  <div class="redoc-response-body">If <code>status</code> is <code>Pending</code>, poll Get request status.</div>
</details>

<details class="redoc-response redoc-response-error">
  <summary><span class="redoc-code">400</span> Validation failed</summary>
  <div class="redoc-response-body">Empty <code>items</code> or a missing <code>available</code> flag.</div>
</details>

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl -X PUT http://ext-integration.haat-delivery.io/api/pos/business/1021/availability \
  -H "X-Api-Key: YOUR_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"items":["ITEM-001","ITEM-002"],"available":false}'
```
</RequestExample>

<ResponseExample>
```json 200
{"requestId":"6651a3f2e1b2c3d4e5f60001","status":"Success","message":"Updated successfully"}
```
</ResponseExample>

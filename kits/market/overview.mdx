# Market overview

Use this kit for **grocery / market** partners (barcodes, weighable items, promotions).

Each endpoint page in the sidebar shows **headers, body fields, and example responses** on the right.

## Who does what

| Direction | Who hosts | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Catalog + Orders | Partner POS | HAAT reads menu and promotions, then submits orders |
| Sync to HAAT | HAAT | Partner logs in and pushes price and availability |

## How to read an endpoint

1. Open the page in the left menu (for example **Build menu**).
2. Read the required headers and fields in the middle.
3. Use the sample request and `200` / error responses on the right.

## Start here

1. [Setup](/kits/market/setup) — tokens and environments
2. **Catalog** — `GET /api/menu/build`, `GET /api/promotions`
3. **Orders** — `POST /api/submit-order`
4. **Sync to HAAT** — login, then `PATCH /api/external/products`
5. [Checklist](/kits/market/checklist) — go-live

<Info>
  Not a grocery market? Use [Restaurant](/kits/restaurant/overview) or [DaaS](/kits/daas/overview) instead.
</Info>

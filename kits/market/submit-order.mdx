---
title: "Submit order"
description: "Accept a customer order from HAAT into the market POS."
api: "POST https://pos.example.com/api/submit-order"
---

HAAT posts the order to **your** POS.

**Example:**

<pre class="redoc-example"><code>integrator-token: YOUR_INTEGRATOR_TOKEN
organization-token: YOUR_ORGANIZATION_TOKEN</code></pre>

## Responses

<details class="redoc-response" open>
  <summary><span class="redoc-code">200</span> Order accepted</summary>
  <div class="redoc-response-body">Return <code>posOrderId</code> from your POS.</div>
</details>

<details class="redoc-response redoc-response-error">
  <summary><span class="redoc-code">401</span> Missing or invalid tokens</summary>
  <div class="redoc-response-body">Validate both tokens before accepting the order.</div>
</details>

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl -X POST https://pos.example.com/api/submit-order \
  -H "integrator-token: YOUR_INTEGRATOR_TOKEN" \
  -H "organization-token: YOUR_ORGANIZATION_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"Items":[{"Code":76868,"SalePrice":12.5,"Quantity":2,"Units":2,"Total":25}],"Payments":[{"Amount":25,"PaymentType":2}],"Total":25}'
```
</RequestExample>

<ResponseExample>
```json 200
{"status":"success","message":"Order accepted","posOrderId":"MKT-78456"}
```
</ResponseExample>

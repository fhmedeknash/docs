---
title: "Sync products"
description: "Push price and availability from the market POS into HAAT."
api: "PATCH http://ext-integration.haat-delivery.io/api/external/products"
---

Call this after [Login](/kits/market/login). Full list once a day, plus a smaller list about every 2 hours.

**Example:**

<pre class="redoc-example"><code>Authorization: Bearer eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9...</code></pre>

<div class="redoc-schema-label">REQUEST BODY SCHEMA: <strong>application/json</strong></div>

<p>Send an <strong>array</strong> of products.</p>

## Responses

<details class="redoc-response" open>
  <summary><span class="redoc-code">200</span> Products updated</summary>
  <div class="redoc-response-body">HAAT confirms which product codes were updated.</div>
</details>

<details class="redoc-response redoc-response-error">
  <summary><span class="redoc-code">401</span> Missing or invalid token</summary>
  <div class="redoc-response-body">Login again and retry with a fresh bearer token.</div>
</details>

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl -X PATCH http://ext-integration.haat-delivery.io/api/external/products \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '[{"productCode":"346960","isAvailable":true,"price":16,"discountPrice":16}]'
```
</RequestExample>

<ResponseExample>
```json 200
"products updated successfully"
```
</ResponseExample>

# Group 4 — They read our catalog

## KubeGet (restaurants)

The only integration that runs backwards.

- **Who starts it:** KubeGet does. They call `GET /api/external/kubeGet/restaurants/{restaurantId}`.
- **What happens:** We hand them our menu. Each item carries an `Id` (our Haat ID) and a `Sku`, which is the mapped `PosId` when one exists and falls back to the Haat ID when it doesn't.
- **Who maps:** **They do**, on their side, using `Sku`. This is the one case where mapping isn't our job.

# Group 5 — Orders only, no catalog sync

For these, the menu is maintained manually in Haat and mapped by ops in backoffice. We push orders out; nothing flows back into the catalog automatically.

**Restaurants:** Beecomm, PayPo, NetLunch, Elpos, PizzaHut, Presto, Beteavon, Iron, Kaman.

**Markets:** Yango, Erp.

Two details worth knowing:

- **PayPo and Beecomm** do send us **order status callbacks** — just not menu data.
- **Presto** is the odd one out on transport: we deliver its **orders over FTP** by uploading an order file, rather than calling an HTTP API.

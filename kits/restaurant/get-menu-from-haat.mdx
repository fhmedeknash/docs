---
title: "Get menu from HAAT"
description: "Read the menu HAAT stores for a business."
api: "GET http://ext-integration.haat-delivery.io/api/pos/business/{businessId}/menu"
---

Read-only. You call HAAT with `X-Api-Key` and get the menu for one business.

For the reverse flow (HAAT pulls from your POS), see [Get menu from POS](/kits/restaurant/get-menu).

### Notes

- All ids are **HAAT** ids (`haatId`), not POS ids.
- Resolve `allocationId` against top-level `allocations`.
- Localized `name` objects use `en-US`, `ar`, `he`, `fr` (and any other stored languages).
- Hidden / deleted items are omitted. Out-of-stock items stay with `available: false`.
- No `requestId` — this is not a sync write.

## Responses

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="200 — Menu">
    `{ lastModified, allocations, categories }`
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="401 / 403 — Auth failed">
    Missing, invalid, or unauthorized `X-Api-Key`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="404 — Business not found">
    Unknown `businessId`.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

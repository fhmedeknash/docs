---
title: "Get menu from POS"
description: "HAAT fetches the full restaurant menu from your POS."
api: "GET https://pos.example.com/api/menu"
---

Return the **full** menu. Keep `posId` stable. `en` is required on names.

**Example:**

<pre class="redoc-example"><code>Authorization: Bearer eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9...
X-Haat-Version: 1.0</code></pre>

## Responses

<details class="redoc-response" open>
  <summary><span class="redoc-code">200</span> Menu successfully retrieved</summary>
  <div class="redoc-response-body">Full menu: categories, meals, deals, and addons.</div>
</details>

<details class="redoc-response redoc-response-error">
  <summary><span class="redoc-code">401</span> Invalid or missing authentication token</summary>
  <div class="redoc-response-body">Check Bearer or <code>X-Signature</code>.</div>
</details>

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl https://pos.example.com/api/menu \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_TOKEN" \
  -H "X-Haat-Version: 1.0"
```
</RequestExample>

[{"posId":1,"name":{"en":"Burgers"},"meals":[{"posId":101,"name":{"en":"Classic Burger"},"price":32.5,"availability":true}],"deals":[]}]

</ResponseExample>

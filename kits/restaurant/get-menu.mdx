---
title: "Get menu from POS"
description: "HAAT fetches the full restaurant menu from your POS."
api: "GET https://pos.example.com/api/menu"
---

Return the **full** menu as an **array of categories**. Keep `PosId` stable. `en-US` is required on names.

**Example:**

<pre class="redoc-example"><code>Authorization: Bearer eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9...
X-Haat-Version: 1.0</code></pre>

## Responses

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="200 — Menu successfully retrieved">
    Full menu: categories with `Meals`, `Deals`, `Groups`, and `MealContents`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="401 — Invalid or missing authentication token">
    Check Bearer or `X-Signature`.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl https://pos.example.com/api/menu \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_TOKEN" \
  -H "X-Haat-Version: 1.0"
```
</RequestExample>

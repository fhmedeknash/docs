# POS for builders

This guide is for **people who build menus** in HAAT — Market builders and Restaurant builders.

## What is a POS?

**POS** means the system the business already uses in the store.

- In a **restaurant**, it is the cash register / kitchen screen (for example Tabit).
- In a **market**, it is the system that holds products, barcodes, and prices (for example Comax).

When someone orders on HAAT, the order appears on **their** screen.

## The three jobs a POS can do

Not every POS does all three.

| Job | In simple words | Almost every POS? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Send them the order** | Customer orders on HAAT → their kitchen / cashier sees it | Yes |
| **Send us catalog info** | They send us menu, prices, or stock. This is what people call “the sync” | Yes |
| **Read our catalog** | They copy our menu. **KubeGet** and **Kaman** do this | No |

## What you must get right

This is the whole job in two lines.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Restaurant builder" icon="utensils">
    Every item that can be ordered has a **PosId** — meal, deal, and ingredient. Tabit and McDonald's often fill this for you.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Market builder" icon="store">
    Copy the **barcode** and **product code** exactly as they are in the POS file. There is no pairing table.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
  If the number is wrong, the customer can still order on HAAT — but the shop will see the wrong item, or “item not found.”
</Warning>

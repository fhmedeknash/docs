# Matching keys

HAAT and the POS each have their own item numbers.

Example: they call a burger **#58821**. We call it **#4417**. Sync and orders only work if we know those two are the **same item**.

How we do that is different for restaurants and markets.

## Restaurants = PosId

Think of **PosId** as “their item number,” saved next to our item in HAAT.

You (or the first menu sync) fill this in for:

- Meals
- Deals
- Toppings
- Groups

When a customer orders, HAAT **changes our numbers into their numbers**, then sends the order.

<Warning>
  If a meal is live on HAAT with **no PosId**, the customer can buy it, and the kitchen will not know what it is. Map everything that can be ordered — including toppings.
</Warning>

### Who fills in PosId?

| POS | Who puts the number in? |
| --- | --- |
| **Tabit** or **McDonald's** (first full menu) | **Automatic** — created with the menu |
| **BeecommCloud**, **LeenCopot**, Beecomm, PayPo, PizzaHut, Iron, and similar | **You**, before go-live |
| **KubeGet**, **Kaman** | **They** do it on their side |

## Markets = barcode and product code

Markets have **no pairing table**. We find the product by the code written on it.

| Code you copy | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| **Barcode** | Finding the product (Comax, Aviv) |
| **Product code** | Finding the product (All4Shops, Barak) **and** attaching **deals** |

Copy the value **exactly** from their file. Do not drop a zero. Do not add a space.

<Warning>
  Deals use **product code**, not barcode. If the product code on the deal does not match the file, the promotion will not work.
</Warning>

## Who does the matching?

**We do** — except **KubeGet** and **Kaman**, who match on their side.

## Before you finish a build

<Check>The business has a POS certificate (it is really connected).</Check>
<Check>You have their item list with **numbers**, not only names or a photo.</Check>
<Check>Restaurants: every item that can be ordered has a PosId.</Check>
<Check>Markets: barcode and product code match the file character by character.</Check>
<Check>You placed a **test order** and the shop screen shows the same items.</Check>
<Check>When they change items later, you update HAAT the same day.</Check>
